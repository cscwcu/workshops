# Success in Computer Science

#### Author(s): Michael Imerman, Daniel Aoulou (Computer Science Club at Western Carolina University)

Last revised: 02-03-2025

## Description

This seminar is designed to help new and upcoming CS students be successful at WCU. Software, tips,
and tricks will be provided to outline how we have been successful and how we can pass that down.
Most of the points will be from personal experience and/or preference from what we have observed
to be effective.
