## 4 WCU Resources

[Home](README.md)

This section will cover resources that are available to students at Western Carolina University. These resources are
used by students at all levels to help them succeed in their classes.

## 4.1 Math and Computer Science Tutoring Center

The Math and Computer Science Tutoring Center (MTC) is one of the best resources for students in need of tutoring. The MTC offers **free** drop-in tutoring for students in a variety of math and computer science courses, no appointment needed!. Tutors are upper-level students who have excelled in the courses they tutor.

Additionally, the MTC offers:

-   A library of math and computer science books and resources
-   One-on-one course-specific tutoring by appointment
-   Calculator loans
-   Class visits to 100-level math and computer science courses
-   A welcoming, social atmosphere for students to study and work on homework

**The MTC is located in Stillwell 437 and is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**. Learn more about the MTC [here](https://www.wcu.edu/learn/academic-success/tutoring-services/services-resources/mathematics-tutoring-center/index.aspx)!

## 4.2 Writing and Learning Commons

The Writing and Learning Commons offers free one-on-one writing tutoring for students at any level and at any point in the writing process. Tutors are trained to help students with any writing assignment, from essays to research papers to resumes. Whether you need helping with generating ideas, formatting, or grammar, the Writing and Learning Commons is here to help!

You must set an appointment to meet with a tutor. Appointments can be made online or in person at the Writing and Learning Commons, located in Belk 207. The Writing and Learning Commons is open Monday through Thursday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Learn more about the Writing and Learning Commons [here](https://www.wcu.edu/learn/academic-success/tutoring-services/services-resources/writing-support/)!

## 4.3 Professors

Professors are one of the best resources available to students. They are experts in their field and are there to help you succeed. Professors have office hours where you can meet with them one-on-one to ask questions, get help with assignments, or discuss course material.

Additionally, WCU CS professors have an open-door policy, meaning you can stop by their office anytime they are available, and their door is open.

All professors' office hours are posted on their syllabi. Additionally, professors keep their schedules updated on their personal websites:

-   [Dr. Holliday](https://agora.cs.wcu.edu/~holliday/)
-   [Dr. Barlowe](https://agora.cs.wcu.edu/~sbarlowe/index.html)
-   [Dr. Scott](https://agora.cs.wcu.edu/~ascott/index.shtml)
-   [Dr. Kreahling](https://agora.cs.wcu.edu/~wck/)

If you are struggling in a class, don't hesitate to reach out to your professor for help. They want to see you succeed!

## 4.4 Advisement

Advisement is a crucial resource for students. Your advisor is there to help you plan your schedule, choose classes, and make sure you are on track to graduate. They can also help you with any academic or personal issues you may be facing.

Most of the time, your advisor will be a professor in the Computer Science department. You can find your advisor's contact information on your MyWCU account. If you are unsure who your advisor is, you can contact the Computer Science department for assistance.

## 4.5 Center for Career and Professional Development

The Center for Career and Professional Development (CCPD) is a valuable resource for students looking to prepare for life after graduation. The CCPD offers services such as:

-   Major and career exploration
-   Resume and cover letter writing
-   Interview preparation
-   Job and internship search assistance
-   Graduate school preparation

The CCPD also hosts career fairs, networking events, and workshops throughout the year to help students connect with potential employers and build their professional skills.

The CCPD is located at 150 Reid and is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Learn more about the CCPD [here](https://www.wcu.edu/learn/academic-enrichment/ccpd/).

## WCU Math and Computer Science Server

The WCU Math and Computer Science Discord server is a great resource for students in the program. The server is a place where students can ask questions, get help with assignments, and connect with other students in the program. The server is also a great place to find out about events, workshops, and other opportunities in the program.
