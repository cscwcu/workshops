# 2 - Choosing the right projects

## What makes a good project?

When choosing a project to work on, it's important to consider a few things. Taking the time to think about these considerations could be the difference between a good project and a great project:

1. **Interest**: Think about a topic you're interested in. If you're not interested in the project, you're less likely to put in the time and effort needed to make it great. Passion is a
   powerful motivator for putting effort into something.
2. **Skills**: Projects should help you develop new skills or improve existing ones. If you're looking to learn a new technology, consider choosing a project that will give you the
   opportunity to work with that technology. Building a project for the sole purpose of learning a new skill is also a great idea!
3. **Impact**: Choose a project that will have a positive impact on others. Projects that solve real-world problems or make people's lives easier are often more rewarding than projects that
   don't have a clear purpose. These types of projects also show employers that you have real world problem solving skills.

## Types of projects

There are many different types of projects that you can work on. Here are a few examples:

1. **Open source projects**: Contributing to open source projects is a great way to gain experience working on real-world projects and to build a portfolio. Open source projects are also a great
   way to learn from others and to get feedback on your work.
2. **Personal projects**: Personal projects are projects that you work on in your free time, outside of school or work. These projects can be anything that interests you, from building a website
   to creating a mobile app.
3. **School projects**: School projects are projects that you work on as part of your coursework. While school projects may not always be the most exciting projects to work on, they can still be
   valuable additions to your portfolio.
4. **Freelance projects**: Freelance projects are projects that you work on for clients. These projects can be a great way to gain experience working with clients and to build a portfolio of
   work that you can show to potential employers.

## Conclusion

Choosing the right projects to work on is an important step in building a portfolio. By choosing projects that you're interested in, that help you develop new skills, and that have a positive
impact on others, you can create a portfolio that showcases your talents and interests.
