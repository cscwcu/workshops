# Projects and Portfolios Seminar

#### Author(s): Daniel Aoulou (Computer Science Club at Western Carolina University)

Last revised: 03-05-2025

## Description

This seminar is designed to help students in computer science and related fields to develop their skills in working on projects and building portfolios. The seminar will cover topics such as
why projects are important, how to choose a project, how to present a project, and how to build a portfolio. The seminar will also discuss tools and technologies for quickly putting together
side projects and portfolios. Lastly, the seminar will provide tips on how to include school projects in a portfolio.
