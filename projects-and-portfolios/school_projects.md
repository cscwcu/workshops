## Including school projects in your portfolio

School projects are a great way to showcase your skills and interests to potential employers. While school projects may not always be the most exciting projects to work on, they can still be valuable additions to your portfolio. Here are a few tips for including school projects in your portfolio:

-   **Choose your best work**: When including school projects in your portfolio, choose the projects that you're most proud of. These are the projects that showcase your skills and interests the best. If a project was particularly challenging or rewarding, it's a good candidate for your portfolio.
-   **Explain the project**: When presenting a school project in your portfolio, make sure to explain what the project is, what technologies you used, and what you learned from it. This will help potential employers understand the project and your role in it.
-   **Highlight your role**: Make sure to highlight your role in the project. If you worked on a team, explain what your responsibilities were and how you contributed to the project.
-   **Academic integrity first**: Make sure that you're following your school's academic integrity policies when including school projects in your portfolio. Many times, making your school projects public can be a violation of academic integrity policies. This most commmonly applies to your code; showing off the functionality of a project is typically okay. If you're unsure, ask your professor or academic advisor.

There are also ways to display private repositories on your GitHub profile. You can use **GitHub's [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/)** feature to create a website that showcases your projects. This way, you can include your school projects in your portfolio without making the code public.

**[gitfront.io](https://gitfront.io/)** is another tool that allows you to showcase your private repositories on your GitHub profile. It's a great way to show off your work without making your code public.
