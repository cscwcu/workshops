## Personal sites

Personal websites are a great way for you to showcase your work and your skills. They can be a central location for potential employers to learn more about you and your projects.
Ideally, a personal websie is a one-stop-shop for all things you.

There are some things you definitely want to include on your personal website:

-   **About me**: A short bio about yourself. This is a great place to talk about your interests, your skills, and your goals.
-   **Projects**: A list of projects that you've worked on. Include a brief description of each project, the technologies you used, and any links to the project.
-   **Resume**: A copy of your resume. This is a great way for potential employers to learn more about your work experience and your skills.
-   **Contact information**: Make it easy for potential employers to get in touch with you. Include an email address or a contact form.

There are also other things that aren't necessary, but can help make your personal website stand out:

-   **Blog**: A blog is a great way to share your thoughts and ideas with the world. It's also a great way to show potential employers that you're passionate about what you do.
-   **Skills**: A list of your skills and technologies you're familiar with. This is a great way to show potential employers what you're capable of at a glance.
-   **Testimonials**: If you've worked with clients or employers in the past, include testimonials from them on your website. This is a great way to show potential employers that you're reliable and easy to work with.

## Portfolios

A standalone portfolio can replace the **Projects** section of your personal website. It's a more focused collection of your work, and it's a great way to show off your best projects.

When building a portfolio, it's important to choose projects that showcase your skills and interests. Here are a few tips for building a great portfolio:

1. **Quality over quantity**: Choose a few projects that you're really proud of, rather than including every project you've ever worked on.
2. **Showcase your skills**: Make sure that your portfolio includes projects that demonstrate your skills. If you're a web developer, include a few web projects. If you're a mobile developer, include a few mobile projects.
3. **Tell a story**: Use your portfolio to tell a story about yourself and the projects you've worked on. Explain why you chose each project, what you learned from it, and how it showcases your skills.
4. **Keep it up to date**: Make sure that your portfolio is up to date with your latest projects. Potential employers want to see what you're currently working on.

## Conclusion

Personal websites and portfolios are great ways to showcase your work and your skills to potential employers. By including an **About me** section, a **Projects** section, and a **Resume** on your personal website, and by choosing a few high-quality projects for your portfolio, you can create a professional online presence that will impress employers.
