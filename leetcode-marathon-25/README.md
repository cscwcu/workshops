## The Competition

The marathon is divided into two brackets: **Underclassmen** and **Upperclassmen**.

**Underclassmen**: Graduating Fall 2026 or later.
**Upperclassmen**: Graduating Spring 2026 or earlier.

Work in teams of up to two students to earn points. This marathon is a collaborative effort to solve LeetCode problems and improve our coding skills together. The goal is to complete as many problems as possible within the given time frame.

The competition will run from **5 PM** to **8 PM** on **April 11, 2025**. During this time, you can work on the problems at your own pace. The winner will be announced at the end of the marathon.

## Prizes

The winner of the Underclassmen bracket will receive a **$50** Amazon gift card.  
The winner of the Upperclassmen bracket will receive an **$80** Amazon gift card.

## Problems

The problems for the marathon are from LeetCode and are assigned points based on their difficulty level. The points are as follows:

### Underclassmen Bracket

-   **Easy**: 2 point
-   **Medium**: 3 points
-   **Hard**: 5 points

### Upperclassmen Bracket

-   **Easy**: 1 point
-   **Medium**: 3 points
-   **Hard**: 5 points

There is a preset list of problems. **Only submissions to the problems in the list will be counted towards your score.** You can find them [here](problems.md)!

## Submissions

We've built a [Submission Portal](https://csc-rtcsp.onrender.com/) to make it easy to make your submissions and see the leaderboard:

[https://csc-rtcsp.onrender.com/](https://csc-rtcsp.onrender.com/)

To make a submission, you'll need a [LeetCode account](https://leetcode.com/accounts/login/). Once you have an account, you can submit your solutions to the problems in the list. Please make sure to follow the submission guidelines below:

1. **Solve the problem**: Choose a problem from the list and solve it using your preferred programming language.
2. **Submit your solution**: Once you have a working solution, submit it on LeetCode.
3. **Copy your submission link**
    - After solving the problem, click on the green Solution button on the top right corner of the tab.
    - Add a title to the soolution and click on the Post button.
    - Copy the URL to your solution.
4. **Submit your link**: Submit your username and the link to the solution on the Submission Portal.

If you're stuck, refer to the video below for a step-by-step guide on how to submit your solution:

[![Submission Guide](https://img.youtube.com/vi/DMK6c0RsoUY/0.jpg)](https://youtu.be/DMK6c0RsoUY)

## Rules

-   You may work in teams of up to two students.
-   You may only submit solutions to the problems in the list.
-   You may do research to understand the mechanics of a problem, but you may not copy code from other sources.
-   The use of Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) is not allowed.
-   You may not work with anyone outside of your team.
